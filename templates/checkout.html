MTN Mobile Money

Steps to complete a MoMo payment
MoMo
  1. Place your order here first so the system generates your order number and total.
  2. Open your MTN MoMo app or USSD menu on your phone.
  3. Select the option to send money or pay a merchant, depending on your handset menu.
  4. Use the payment details provided by EMG support and enter the exact total of {{ grand_total }} RWF.
  5. Use your order number as the payment reference when your phone asks for a note.
  6. Confirm the payment with your PIN and keep the confirmation SMS for your records.

Mobile menu names can differ slightly by phone. If you need help, call or WhatsApp 0789 670 931.

Airtel Money

Steps to complete an Airtel payment
Airtel
  1. Place the order here first so your final amount and order number are recorded.
  2. Open Airtel Money on your phone through the app or your Airtel menu.
  3. Choose the option to send money or pay a merchant.
  4. Enter the payment details given by EMG support and confirm the exact amount of {{ grand_total }} RWF.
  5. Add your order number as the payment reason if the wallet prompts for a note.
  6. Approve the transfer with your PIN and save the confirmation message until delivery is completed.

If you get stuck on any step, contact 0789 670 931 and the team will guide you.
